17 · Measure before and after you ship

Evaluations and AI red teaming

Evaluation measures the quality, safety and reliability of models and agents throughout development and production. An evaluation applies one or more evaluators to your data or application, producing scores and explanations that let you compare versions and decide if something is ready.

AI red teaming

The AI Red Teaming Agent uses Microsoft's open-source PyRIT framework plus Foundry's risk and safety evaluators to automate three things: scan (simulated adversarial probing), evaluate (score each attack-response pair into an Attack Success Rate) and report (a scorecard you can track over time in Foundry).

  • Use it at design (pick the safest model), development (upgrade or fine-tune), pre-deployment, and post-deployment with scheduled continuous red-teaming on synthetic adversarial data.
  • Supports models and agents, local and cloud runs; text-based scenarios only. Categories include hateful and unfair, sexual, violent, self-harm, protected materials, code vulnerability and ungrounded attributes.
  • It complements, not replaces, guardrails: in production keep content filters and safety system messages, and use the control plane to govern agent fleets.
Make it a habit. Establish a baseline score first, so every later change (a new prompt, model, tool or knowledge source) is measured rather than anecdotal. Then wire the same dataset into the optimizer and your CI pipeline.
