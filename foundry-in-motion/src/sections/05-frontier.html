05 · Frontier models

Frontier model access inside Foundry

Frontier models arrive in Foundry from several providers and with different access paths: some are available to everyone on Global Standard, some need a Marketplace subscription, some need a registration request, and some are gated research previews.

Names and availability change quickly. The lineup below reflects the Microsoft Learn model pages read in October 2026. Treat it as a map of families and access paths, and confirm exact model IDs, regions and lifecycle on the linked pages before presenting.

The access ladder

Not every model needs every step. Work down this list and stop when the model is deployable:

Subscribing to partner models

You need Azure Marketplace permissions such as Microsoft.MarketplaceOrdering/agreements/offers/plans/sign/action and Microsoft.SaaS/resources/write to subscribe. Many organizations give this to a small group, so plan for it early.

Quota is per subscription and model

Claude quota is managed at the subscription level: all Global Standard deployments of one model share a single pool across regions. Request more through the quota form (also on the Foundry quota page); approvals can take several days.
